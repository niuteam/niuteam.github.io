01 / About

Curiosity across disciplines

BEAM Team is an academic interest group associated with the Beihang University academic community. We aim to encourage peer learning, open discussions, knowledge sharing, and exploration of emerging technologies in biomedical engineering.

Our perspectiveMeaningful ideas often emerge at the boundaries between fields. We are interested in understanding biological systems through signals, waves, imaging, and computational methods.

02 / Research Interests

What we explore

Our academic interests span sensing, signal analysis, imaging, and intelligent interfaces for health and communication.

01

Biomedical Acoustics

Acoustic sensing and the interaction of sound waves with biological systems.

02

Ultrasound & Imaging

Ultrasound methods, image formation, and wearable imaging technologies.

03

Physiological Signals

Acquisition, processing, and interpretation of human physiological signals.

04

Multimodal Interfaces

Intelligent sensing, speech-related technologies, and multimodal learning.
