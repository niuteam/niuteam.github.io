01 / ABOUT

A community built around
questions worth exploring.

NIU Team is an academic research community at Beihang University, with interests spanning biomedical engineering, acoustics, and multimodal technologies.

We value interdisciplinary exchange and careful experimentation. By bringing together ideas from signal processing, medical imaging, sensing, and speech technologies, we explore how research methods can connect to real human needs.

Based in Beijing, China · Beihang University
