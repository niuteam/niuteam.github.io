01 / About us

Curiosity across disciplines.

NIU Team is an academic research community at Beihang University, focusing on biomedical engineering, acoustics, and multimodal technologies.

Our interests span biomedical ultrasound, medical imaging, physiological signal processing, wearable sensing, and speech-related biomedical applications.

We aim to cultivate an open and collaborative environment for research discussions, knowledge sharing, interdisciplinary exchange, and hands-on learning.

Connecting biomedical needs with innovative sensing and intelligent engineering.Our perspective
02 / What we explore

Research interests.

Learning representations and extracting useful information from complex physiological measurements.

Exploring flexible sensors, wearable systems, and complementary information from multiple modalities.

Investigating speech-related physiology and technologies that could support human communication.
